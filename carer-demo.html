The problem

Who visits whom, when, and to do what.

A home-care agency sends staff to clients' homes every day. Each client has a care plan: meals, housekeeping and care tasks on set days. Visits repeat on rotations, and every shift needs the right person on it.

  • Care plans change week to week, per client and per day.
  • Shifts follow rotations, so one change can move many visits.
  • Staff records need documents and emergency contacts kept with them.

The product

Three modules on one backend.

Access

Tenants, users, roles, features, packages and sessions, with JWT sign-in.

CRM

Clients and their addresses, care plans by day, food and housekeeping activities, care to-dos, shifts and staff assignments.

HRM

Staff, their documents and emergency contacts, departments and designations.
