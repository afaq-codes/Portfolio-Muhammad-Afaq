The problem

Many practices, one billing team.

A billing team works for many practices at once. Every claim has to be sent, chased and paid. Every denial needs a next step. Each practice should see only its own patients and claims.

The team also runs its own day: calls with payers, mail, requests from practices, staff attendance and payroll. CareMD puts all of that in one product, with permission checks on the server.

  • Each practice's data is kept separate.
  • The next follow-up for each claim is created on schedule, without duplicates.
  • Practices send requests and get answers inside the product.

What I built

The parts the team uses every day.

Live in daily use. Shipped deployed to production. Built done, not deployed yet.

Payment ledger

Insurance payments are posted per claim and per CPT line in one workspace, with a running balance. A balance sheet check that compares posted payments was committed on 11 Nov 2026 and isn’t deployed yet.

Live

AR follow-up engine

Each insurer has its own follow-up schedule. CareMD creates the next follow-up for every claim and escalates it, with no duplicates when it runs again. A team lead assigns the work.

ShippedTest on a data copy: 1,653 follow-ups, 0 duplicates

Denial management

Denials are tracked as events with a cap on resubmissions. They sit in one AR Workspace, next to the follow-up queue.

Shipped

RingCentral phone

Calls sync into CareMD with recordings, a call report, voicemail, texts and fax. A newer part transcribes calls with Groq and writes AI call notes onto the matching call.

LiveAI notes: built

Client portal

Practices send requests to the billing team, chat on each request and get web push alerts. A response-time clock and coverage outcomes went live in Oct 2026.

Live

Case Flow board

A ClickUp-style board for a neuropsychology clinic: Space, Folder, Stage and Case, with labels, short case keys, one assignee and an activity feed. Stage deadlines came in Nov 2026.

Live

Neuro referrals

Referrals come in, a doctor decides, and an accepted referral opens a case on the Case Flow board.

Shipped

Payer Intelligence

Payer, plan and coverage data, carved out as its own app inside CareMD with separate screens and permissions.

Shipped

Medical record submissions

Records sent to payers are logged per claim, with a daily productivity count. Record emails and PDF files are built but not deployed yet.

Shipped

DME as its own flow

Durable medical equipment work has its own flow, dashboard and reports, apart from the main billing flow.
