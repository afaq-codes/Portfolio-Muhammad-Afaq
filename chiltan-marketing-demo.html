Overview

One app, many stores.

A store builds an email in a drag-and-drop editor, picks who gets it, and sends it by email, SMS or push. The app also messages shoppers who left a Shopify checkout, and tracks opens and clicks.

Each store needs its own staff roles, its own mail settings and only its own data. That was my part.

SMTP and store settings

Brands on campaigns
