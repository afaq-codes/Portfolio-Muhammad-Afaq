The problem

Shop, website and marketplace sales lived apart.

A retailer sells over the counter, on its own store and on marketplaces like Daraz. Stock, orders, couriers and cash end up in different tools, and the books are hard to match.

RynoDesk keeps all of it in one system: the POS, products and inventory, orders and dispatch, a journal ledger, CRM and reports. It runs in the browser and as an Electron desktop app.

  • Orders and dispatch
  • Products and BOM
  • Inventory: PO, GRN, transfers, audit
  • Chart of accounts
  • Journal vouchers

  • Users, roles, stores

Integrations

One shape for every storefront.

Every platform plugs into one integration factory with the same five calls. A new marketplace is one more class that answers those five.

  • eBay: 35 files, about 1,700 lines, May 2026.
  • Etsy: 16 files, about 800 lines, June 2026.
  • Wix: 9 commits to finish it, 38 files, about 1,800 lines, May 2026.
  • Courier returns and COD remittance: 68 files, about 2,600 lines, June to July 2026.

To be clear: eBay, Etsy, Wix, courier returns and COD remittance are on feature branches, not on main. The 10 Pakistani courier integrations on main (TCS, Leopard, PostEx, Trax and others) were built by my teammates.
