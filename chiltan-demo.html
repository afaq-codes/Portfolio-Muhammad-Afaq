Overview

Many ways to sell, one system.

The business makes its own products and sells them online, by courier, and in its own shops. Those orders, the stock behind them, production and staff all sit in one ERP and one database.

127tables in the ERP database, Nov 2024 (whole system)

What it covers

Six areas.

Online orders

Order status history, courier bookings and tracking, riders and order sync jobs.

Shop tills

Store orders, payments and returns, cash entries and a day close.

Stock

Products with variants, barcodes and stickers, store stock and audits.

Production

Recipes, raw materials, manufacturing requests and material use.

Purchasing

Vendors, purchase orders and returns, and demand sheets.

People

Employees, attendance, allowances, users, roles and role groups.

Where this comes from: the system's database schema (Nov 2024). It shows what the ERP covers, not a list of what I built.
