AI inside the product

Assistants that do real work, with limits.

I add AI where it saves someone a click or a call. The model gets tools it can use, a spend cap, and a person to hand over to.

  • FamTill Live

    Claude Haiku with 34 read-only tools. Typed or spoken questions about sales, stock, repairs and VAT come back as tiles, tables and charts.

  • Orderyfy Live

    7 tools that search products, fill the real cart and place the order. A cached prompt, a monthly budget guard, and a team inbox for questions it can't answer.

  • OmniStock

    Claude Agent SDK with 12 read-only tools. Its SQL runs in a separate worker that only allows SELECT.

  • RynoChat

    A flow builder with LLM and handoff steps, one provider layer over Claude, Groq and Gemini, and voice notes turned into text.

  • CareMD Built

    Groq transcribes recorded calls, and AI call notes land on the matching call record.
