The problem

Every number feeds the next one.

A call-centre sales business has many moving parts. Fronters and closers work leads. QA checks the calls. A sale decides a commission, a chargeback takes it back, and attendance decides the pay.

Nixxe CRM keeps all of that in one product, so sales, QA, HR and payroll read from the same records.

  • Leads arrive from Google Sheets and a master import, and each sale is attributed to an agent and a team.
  • Targets, QA scores and improvement plans sit next to the sales they measure.
  • Payroll reads attendance, leave and sales rules from the same data.

What I built

From the first call to the payslip.

Cards marked "New" came after the split in Sep 2026.

Lead pipeline and attribution

Google Sheets lead sync and a master import. Closer and fronter roles, blacklist lookup, lead transfer and a daily sales export.

Commissions and sales reports

Commission reports by team side, sales reports by team and agent, team-lead totals and sales report export.

QA scorecards

QA forms with sections and questions, separate fronting and closing forms, reviews with answers, a mistake list and QA assignment for closers.

New

Targets and improvement plans

Monthly targets per employee or team, a KPI overview, flag bands, dependability, and leave and cancellation stats. PIPs track expectations, check-ins, extensions and outcomes.

New

Support and retention

Winbacks and chargebacks, customer follow-ups, support outcomes and inbound call logging for the support team.

Hiring pipeline

Hiring leads from Meta lead forms and other sources move through an interview pipeline with full status history.

Attendance and leave

Schedules with automatic breaks, leave approval windows and partial approval, increment settings and attendance export.

Payroll

Income tax, payroll reconciliation, sandwich days, loan repayment by salary month, Saturday waivers, company off periods and pay rules with a sales condition.

New rules

Activity tracking

Activity types, schedules, enrolments and sessions, idle detection and a "my activity" view of the day.

New

Workforce monitoring

A Windows tray agent plus admin screens inside the CRM. Staff agree to a notice before anything is captured.

The split

From a shared core to its own product.

  1. Built on the CareMD coreThe CRM started inside the shared CareMD codebase, first pushed in Dec 2024. Over that whole history I wrote about 94% of the backend and 88% of the frontend commits.
  2. Split on 22 Sep 2026I moved it into its own backend and frontend repos, kept the CRM modules and started retiring the billing code.
  3. Grew after the splitMigrations V333 to V380 added monitoring, performance, PIPs, QA scorecards, activity tracking and new payroll rules. Every commit since the split is mine: 82 backend, 90 frontend.
