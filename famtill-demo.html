The problem

A phone shop does three jobs at once.

An independent UK phone shop sells new and used phones, sells accessories, and fixes phones in the back room. Each job needs something a plain till does not have.

  • Used phones are one of a kind. Each needs its own IMEI, grade and cost, tracked from purchase to sale.
  • Repairs need a job sheet. Faults, passcode, parts from stock, deposits, and a status the customer can ask about.
  • The books need UK rules. Prices in GBP, 20% VAT, a VAT return and UK GDPR tools.

FamTill puts the counter, the workshop and the books in one web app. A shop opens the till, books in a repair, and the stock and the books update from the same sale.

What I built

One app from the till to the VAT return.

Touch till

Scans barcodes and IMEIs. Split payments, staff discount limits, parked sales, quotes, trade prices, refunds, thermal receipts, X and Z reports.

IMEI stock

Each phone is one record with its own IMEI, grade and cost. One search in the top bar finds a phone across stock, sales and repairs.

Repairs

Book in by IMEI with faults and condition. A status flow from booked in to collected, with parts from stock, labour, deposits, job sheets and warranty.

Inventory

Suppliers, purchase orders, goods received, transfers between shops, stocktakes, write-offs and an Excel import for opening stock.

Double-entry books

UK chart of accounts, journals, expenses and payments. Sales and stock post through one queue with a guard against double posting. P&L, balance sheet, VAT return.

Customers

Purchase and repair history per customer, credit accounts with statements, and a customer dashboard.

Dashboard and reports

A live business dashboard plus sales, stock, repair and money reports, with Excel, PDF and CSV export.

Multi-tenant SaaS

Each business is its own tenant. Several shops with a store switcher, four roles (Owner, Manager, Technician, Till), and plans that set shop and user limits.

UK GDPR

Customer consent, data export, erasure and a complaints log.

Not built yet: trade-in register, margin-scheme VAT, a repair-status page for customers, SMS alerts, card terminals, an offline till and a mobile app. WhatsApp "repair ready" messages are built but switched on for the platform admin only.

AI inside the product

Ask the shop a question. Get a card back.

A chat panel in the top bar. Staff type or speak a question, and the assistant answers from the shop's own data. It runs on Claude Haiku through the Anthropic Java SDK. It went live with the product on 11 Nov 2026.

  • 34 read-only tools

    Sales, stock and phones, customers, repairs and staff, money and VAT, suppliers, and a "where do I…" screen finder. It can show data but never change it.

  • Answers as cards

    Tiles, tables, charts, records, links to the right screen, and exports.

  • Voice in and out

    Speak the question with the browser's speech input. The answer can be read back aloud.

  • Cost limits

    Per-shop budgets, a daily question limit per user, usage metering and saved conversation history.
